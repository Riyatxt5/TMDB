# TMDB

Top 5 Overall Insights & Recommendations
Movie performance varies significantly across genres, making genre an important factor in content planning and investment decisions.
Higher production budgets generally contribute to higher box-office revenue, but high spending does not always guarantee commercial success.
Certain actors and directors have consistently appeared across multiple successful movies, highlighting the importance of experienced talent.
Movie ratings, popularity, and audience engagement show different patterns, suggesting that popularity alone does not always indicate audience reception.
Keyword, genre, and movie trends can help studios and streaming platforms identify popular themes and make better content and marketing decisions.
